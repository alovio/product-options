# WooCommerce Plugin Opportunity — Market Research (May 2026)

Two adversarially-verified deep-research passes. Pass 2 (demand-and-revenue-first,
no skills bias) is authoritative; Pass 1 was biased toward payment skills and is kept
only for context.

## Decision
**Build: WooCommerce Extra Product Options / Product Add-Ons WITH conditional logic.**
Model: freemium (wordpress.org) + Freemius annual-recurring (+ monthly).
Strategy: **A+B hybrid** — primary wedge = *conditional logic in the free tier*;
supporting pillar = *modern visual drag-drop builder with live preview*.

## Why this won (verified, 3-0 unless noted)
- **Primary-source unmet demand:** WooCommerce's own feature-request board —
  "Conditional Logic on WooCommerce Product Add-Ons": **139 votes, 26 comments,
  Status "Open" since 22 Jul 2019**, still unbuilt by the core extension as of May 2026.
  https://woocommerce.com/feature-request/conditional-logic-on-woocommerce-product-add-ons/
- **Explicit willingness-to-pay:** users literally say *"I'm not buying this because it
  has no conditional logic… when it's added I'll be buying it."*
- **Proven freemium + annual paid market:** Studio Wombat *Advanced Product Fields*
  (50,000+ free installs, 4.8★, paid Pro); Plugin Republic *Product Add-Ons Ultimate*
  ($69–119/yr); Acowebs *Custom Product Addons* ($49/yr, freemium); Barn2 *Product Options* (~$79/yr).
- **CodeCanyon signal:** "Extra Product Options & Add-Ons" ~$69; Fancy Product Designer/Lumise
  $59–69 — the top price tier in the WooCommerce category.
- **Low risk:** form/field UI + conditional rules. **No payment processing, no email
  deliverability liability** (unlike deposits or messaging plugins). Moderate solo build scope.
- **Huge addressable market:** WooCommerce 7M+ active installs, ~6M live stores, ~36% of online stores.

## Ranked shortlist (demand × revenue × ease of entry)
1. **Product Add-Ons / Extra Product Options + conditional logic** — STRONGEST (above)
2. **Checkout optimization** — proven annual WTP ($49–199/yr; Fluid Checkout ~€99/yr,
   ThemeHigh Checkout Field Editor $49/99/199/yr); conversion-uplift pitch — but more crowded
3. **Product filtering** — CodeCanyon #1 ("Filter Everything" $49) — harder UX/perf build
4. **Multi-currency** — CodeCanyon #2 ("CURCY" $34) — rate-sync complexity
5. **Product designer/personalization** (Fancy Product Designer $59, Lumise $69, 13–23K sales) —
   highest price + growing print-on-demand market, but materially harder for a solo dev

## AVOID (saturated / incumbent-dominated)
- Email marketing — Mailchimp for WooCommerce 221K+ stores
- Subscriptions / recurring billing — WooCommerce Subscriptions 39K+ stores
- SEO — Yoast ~36–39%
- Forms — WPForms / Gravity Forms
- Page building — Elementor / WPBakery

## Economics / honest caveats
- **Freemium converts at only 3–4%** (verified) → need high install volume. Market scale supports it.
- **Offer monthly alongside annual** — a Freemius experiment showed removing monthly cut
  total signups ~47%.
- **No reliable revenue-ceiling figure survived verification** — several "median plugin earns $X"
  claims were REFUTED (0-3). Do NOT assume a specific income; it is unestablished.
- Figures are May 2026 snapshots; install/share numbers drift.
- Directional white-space per WooCommerce guidance (medium confidence): crypto, dropshipping,
  fraud prevention, localization, analytics, AR/VR, SaaS.
